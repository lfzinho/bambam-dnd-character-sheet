"What Can I Do On My Turn?" (Action Cheat Sheet)

1. Actions (Choose one per turn)

  • Attack: Swing your Greataxe or throw a Handaxe.
  • Dash / Disengage / Dodge / Grapple / Shove: Standard combat actions anyone can do.

2. Bonus Actions (Choose one per turn, if applicable)

  • Enter a Rage: You can do this 3 times per long rest. Source: Barbarian feature. It lasts for 1 minute (10 rounds). It ends early if you are knocked unconscious, or if you end your turn without attacking a hostile creature or taking damage.

3. Passive Abilities & Combat Mechanics

  • Reckless Attack (Barbarian Level 2): When you make your first attack on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. You get Advantage (roll 2d20, keep the highest) on all STR attacks this turn. The Catch: All enemies get Advantage on their attacks against you until your next turn.
  • Totem Spirit: Bear (Barbarian Level 3): While you are Raging, you have Resistance (take half damage) to ALL damage types except psychic damage.
  • Danger Sense (Barbarian Level 2): You have Advantage on DEX saving throws against effects that you can see, such as traps and spells.
  • Relentless Endurance (Half-Orc): When you are reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 HP instead (once per long rest).
  • Savage Attacks (Half-Orc): When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.

Spirit Seeker: BAMBAM's Magic

Because you chose the Totem Warrior path, you can cast these two spells as Rituals (meaning it takes 10 extra minutes to cast them outside of combat).

  • Speak with Animals: (Lasts for 10 minutes). You can hold a conversation with beasts.
  • Beast Sense: (Lasts up to 1 hour). You touch a willing animal. For the duration, you can close your eyes and see through that animal's eyes, and hear what it hears. You are blind and deaf to your own body while doing this.

BAMBAM strictly follows the constitution of his tribe.

  1. Quem levantar a maior coisa é o líder.

Future Progression (Levels 4 & 5)

Level 4: The Skill Expert Upgrade

BAMBAM becomes the ultimate lifter and his core math gets a massive boost.

  • Hit Points (HP): Increases to 41. Math: Previous 32 + 7 for Level 4 + 2 CON modifier = 41.
  • Feat Chosen - Skill Expert:
    • Strength Boost: +1 to Strength, bringing the total score to 18. This upgrades the Strength modifier to +4.
    • Combat Math Update: Because the Strength modifier is now +4, Greataxe attacks now roll with +6 to hit and deal 1d12 + 4 damage. Math: +4 STR + 2 Prof to hit.
    • The "Levante" Champion (Expertise): You gain Expertise in Athletics. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for this skill. Your new Athletics score is a massive +8. Math: +4 STR + 4 Double Prof.
    • New Skill: You gain proficiency in one additional skill of your choice to add to your sheet.

Level 5: The Juggernaut (Sneak Peek)

A quick glance at the massive power spike coming at Level 5.

  • Extra Attack: You can attack twice per turn instead of just once.
  • Fast Movement: Your base walking speed increases by 10 feet (Total: 40 feet), making it impossible for enemies to escape.
  • Proficiency Bonus Increase: Your Proficiency Bonus jumps from +2 to +3. This automatically improves your attack rolls, saving throws, and all skills you are proficient in.
